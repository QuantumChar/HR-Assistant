# Leave and Paid Time Off

> Sample workshop content. Replace and approve it before production use.

## Paid time off

Employees should review the official leave policy for accrual schedules, carryover limits,
eligibility, and required notice. Rules may differ by employment classification and location.

## Requesting leave

Submit leave through the authenticated HR system and follow manager-notification requirements.
The HR Assistant cannot view balances, approve leave, or submit a request.

## Protected or accommodation-related leave

Contact the designated HR team for confidential guidance. Do not provide medical details,
accommodation records, or case information in the assistant chat.
