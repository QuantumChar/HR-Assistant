# Open Enrollment Guide

> Sample workshop content. Replace dates, links, and plan details with approved information.

## Before enrollment

Review the current plan summaries, confirm covered dependents, compare total expected costs, and
check whether preferred providers participate in each network.

## Enrollment process

Complete elections in the authenticated benefits portal before the published deadline. Save the
confirmation provided by the portal. The HR Assistant explains the process but cannot submit or
change an election.

## After the window closes

Changes outside open enrollment generally require a qualifying life event and supporting
documentation under the official plan rules. Contact HR Benefits for case-specific guidance.
