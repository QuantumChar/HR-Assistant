# Workplace Support and Escalation

> Sample workshop content. Replace contacts and procedures with approved information.

## Routine support

Use the HR support channel for policy clarification, workplace concerns, employee-relations
questions, and requests that require a person to review context.

## Confidential matters

Do not enter investigation details, medical information, accommodation records, allegations, or
another employee's personal information in the assistant. Contact the designated confidential
support channel instead.

## Immediate safety concerns

Use the organization's emergency process or local emergency services when anyone may be in
immediate danger. The HR Assistant is not an emergency-response service.
