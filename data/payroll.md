# Payroll Information

> Sample workshop content. Replace and approve it before production use.

## Pay schedule

The official payroll calendar lists pay dates, time-entry deadlines, and holiday adjustments.
Employees should submit and approve time according to the published schedule.

## Updating payment information

Use the authenticated HR system to update direct deposit or tax withholding. The HR Assistant
cannot view pay statements, compensation, banking details, tax forms, or payment status.

## Getting help

Contact Payroll through the approved support channel for a missing payment, correction, or other
employee-specific question. Do not enter bank details, tax identifiers, or pay-statement data in
chat.
