# Benefits Overview

> Sample workshop content. Replace and approve it before production use.

## Available benefit categories

Eligible employees may be offered medical, dental, vision, life insurance, disability coverage,
and retirement benefits. Eligibility and effective dates are governed by the official plan
documents.

## Comparing plans

Compare premiums, deductibles, copayments, coinsurance, provider networks, prescription coverage,
and out-of-pocket maximums. The HR Assistant can explain published terms but cannot recommend or
select a plan for an employee.

## Personal questions

Use the authenticated benefits portal for current elections, covered dependents, claims, and
individual eligibility. Contact the HR Benefits team when the portal information is unclear.
